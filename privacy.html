Privacy Policy - Astral Shapes Tower Defence

Last updated: 9 October 2026

In short. The game has no accounts. Your progress is saved on your phone and is not sent to us. The game collects nothing about you itself. Three outside services touch your data: Google AdMob if you watch an advert, Google Play if you buy something, and Google Forms if you choose to send feedback. This page says what each one receives, and how to say no.

1. Who we are

Astral Shapes Tower Defence is a game for Android phones. It is published by Fission Games Studio. The person behind it is known publicly only as E. "We" in this policy means Fission Games Studio.

We decide what is done with the information that we receive, which this page describes. Google and Discord decide what they do with the information they receive. Their own policies apply to that.

2. What stays on your phone

The game keeps these in its own storage on your phone:

  • A save file, with a backup copy beside it. It holds your progress and choices: levels cleared, upgrades, currencies, cards, cosmetics, settings, and which mailbox messages you have read and claimed. It also holds the times the game needs for its timers and daily limits, the codes you have redeemed, and, in versions with purchases, the products you have bought and their order numbers.
  • A cache of pictures that the game draws for itself, so that it starts faster. The game can rebuild it.

The save holds no name, email address, phone number or location. The game does not send these files to us or to anyone else. There is no cloud save in this version.

The game reads your phone's memory size and number of processor cores, and whether Android's animations are switched off. It uses them to choose sensible settings. They stay on your phone. The game uses the vibration motor if you leave Vibration on in Settings. It does not ask for access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or files.

The mailbox holds messages from us. They are built into each update of the game, and the game does not fetch them from a server. Which ones you have read is kept in your save.

Redeem codes are checked on your phone. Nothing is sent when you enter one. Codes that Google Play gives out are handled by Google Play (see section 5).

If Android backup is switched on, Android may copy the game's save to your Google account. That is an Android feature, and we cannot see the copy.

4. Adverts and consent

Versions with adverts use Google AdMob. Adverts are optional. You choose to watch one, and you get a reward for finishing it. No advert appears between screens. A subscription removes adverts.

AdMob receives the data in the table in section 3. The game's own code does not read your advertising ID. Only AdMob does. Google explains how it uses this data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Consent in the UK and the European Economic Area. If you are there, the game asks Google on every start whether you must be shown a consent message. If you must, Google's consent form (the User Messaging Platform) appears before any advert is loaded. You can say no and keep playing. Adverts shown without your consent are not personalised to you, although Google may still use limited data to count views and prevent fraud.

How to change your answer.

  • In the game, open Settings and tap Privacy choices. This row appears when Google says your answer can be changed, which means in the UK and the European Economic Area.
  • On any phone, open Android's Settings and look under Privacy, then Ads (or Google, then Ads). There you can reset your advertising ID or delete it, and switch off ad personalisation.

5. Purchases

The game is free to play. Versions with purchases let you buy digital items and pay through Google Play. These are Diamond packs, passes, a permanent Coin boost for the Crucible and a monthly ad-free subscription. Your own currency and price are shown before you pay.

Google handles the payment, refunds and cancelling a subscription. To cancel, open Google Play, then Payments and subscriptions, then Subscriptions. The game receives a receipt from Google Play that says you bought a product, and keeps it in your save so that the purchase can be restored. The game does not receive your card or bank details. Google may share limited order details with us, such as the order number, product, date and country, so that we can deal with refunds and questions.

You do not need an account with us to buy or to restore a purchase on a new phone. Restoring uses your Google account.

6. Feedback form and messages

The Feedback row in Settings opens a Google Form in your phone's browser. The game sends nothing itself, except that the link carries the game's version number. You decide what to type.

The form asks for your phone make and model, your Android version, how long you have played, which parts of the game you played, whether it crashed, how it ran, and what you found confusing or enjoyed. It does not ask for your name or email address. Please do not type personal details into the free-text boxes. Google runs the form, and Google's privacy policy applies to it. The answers go to a private spreadsheet that only we can open. We keep them for 12 months.

If you message us in the Discord server or open an issue on our GitHub page (see section 14), we receive your Discord or GitHub name and what you write. We use them to answer you. We keep the message for 12 months.

7. The Discord link

The Community row in Settings opens our Discord server invite in your phone's browser or the Discord app. The game sends nothing to Discord. Once you leave the game and join the server, Discord's own privacy policy and rules apply to you, and Discord collects what it says there. What you post on the server is public to its members. Discord requires its users to be at least 13.

8. Children

The game is for players aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are under 13, please do not use the game.

If you are under 18, please ask a parent or guardian before you buy anything or agree to personalised adverts.

If you are a parent or guardian and think your child under 13 has sent us information, for example through the feedback form or by message, contact us as described in section 14. Tell us roughly when it was sent and what it said. We will look for it, delete it, and reply to confirm. We will not ask you for more than we need to find it.

9. How long we keep things

  • Your save and the picture cache stay on your phone until you remove them. We hold no copy.
  • Feedback form answers and messages to us are kept for 12 months and then deleted.
  • Data held by Google (adverts, payments, Google Play, Google Forms hosting) follows Google's policies. Data held by Discord follows Discord's.

10. Your rights

Under UK and EU data protection law (UK GDPR and EU GDPR) you can ask us for a copy of the personal information we hold about you. You can ask us to correct or delete it, ask us to restrict how we use it, and object to how we use it. You can withdraw any consent you gave.

We rely on your consent for personalised adverts and for the feedback you choose to send. We rely on our legitimate interest in running and improving the game when we answer a message. Because we hold almost nothing that names you, we may need a detail or two to find what is yours.

You can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office in the UK (ico.org.uk) or to the data protection authority in your own country.

California. We do not sell your personal information. California residents can ask us what we hold about them and ask us to delete it, and we will not treat them worse for asking.

11. Deleting your data

  • On your phone. Uninstalling the game removes the save and the picture cache. You can also open Android's Settings, then Apps, then the game, then Storage, and tap Clear storage. The wording differs between phones. If Android backup is on, a copy may sit in your Google account, which you manage in Google's settings.
  • Feedback form answers or messages to us. Contact us as described in section 14. Tell us roughly when you sent it and which version of the game you used, because the form does not know who you are.
  • Your advertising ID. Open Android's Settings, then Privacy, then Ads (or Google, then Ads), and choose to delete or reset it.
  • Data held by Google or Discord. Ask them, through their own tools and policies. We cannot delete it for you.

12. Security

The save is in the game's private storage on your phone. The Google services the game uses connect over encrypted connections. The feedback spreadsheet and our accounts are protected by sign-in, and only we can open them. No system is perfectly safe, so we ask you not to put personal details in feedback.

Google may process data outside the UK and the European Economic Area, under its own safeguards.

13. Changes to this policy

We change this page when the game changes what it collects, and the date at the top changes with it. When a change affects what is collected, we also update the Data safety section of the game's Google Play page.
